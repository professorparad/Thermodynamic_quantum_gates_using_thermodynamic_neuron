Minimal Floquet-buffered thermodynamic NOT experiment
====================================================

Objective:
Compare a direct output-reservoir contact with a Floquet-buffered output-reservoir contact for the smallest NOT-style gate prototype.

Chosen baseline setting:
- epsilon1=20.0
- hot-input output beta=1.999961
- cold-input output beta=1.000053
- baseline output margin=0.999908

Buffered/direct evidence to carry into the prototype:
- default Floquet gain=0.046438
- default drive-work proxy=0.460415
- best ablation case=weak_system_buffer_coupling with gain=0.711315

Required observables:
- final trace distance between logical outputs
- output excited-state population or output beta proxy
- purity and positivity/minimum eigenvalue
- trace and Hermiticity error
- drive-work proxy, labelled provisional

Stop criterion:
Proceed toward the full three-qubit gate only if the buffered prototype improves distinguishability over direct contact and remains converged under time-step and memory/bath-contact checks.
